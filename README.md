## ⌁ About Me

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%" valign="top">

I'm **Amjith**, better known online as **Logan Tempest** — a B.Tech Civil Engineering student who spends most of his free time writing code instead of studying structural analysis.

What started as curiosity about how websites work turned into a genuine obsession with building things — full stack apps, AI-powered tools, and eventually, a startup of my own.

I currently serve as **Student Lead II at IEDC, SNM Institute of Management & Technology**, where I help other students turn ideas into actual products. I also run **Git & GitHub workshops**, because I remember how confusing version control felt the first time, and I like closing that gap for others.

Right now, I split my attention between three worlds that don't usually meet: **civil engineering, artificial intelligence, and startup building** — and I'm slowly figuring out where they intersect.

<div align="center">

I'd rather ship something small that works than something large that impresses no one but me.
